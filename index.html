A Filipino Documentary Feature

Stories That Deserve To Be Seen.

Look closer. Listen closer. Understand more.

Beyond the beauty of the Philippines are stories that often go unnoticed. This page features documentaries that reveal the realities, struggles, and experiences of Filipino communities, bringing attention to issues that deserve to be seen and understood.

I advocate for awareness through storytelling, using documentaries to shed light on the realities of our country that are often overlooked or unheard. I believe that by helping people see these stories, we can encourage Filipinos to listen, learn, question, and understand the issues affecting our communities. Because awareness is a step toward meaningful change.
